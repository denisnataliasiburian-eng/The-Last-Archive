THE LAST ARCHIVE · GENERAL ARCHIVE · 02

GROUP MEMORIES

The moments that became part of the archive. Momen-momen yang akhirnya menjadi bagian dari arsip.
Not every memory was important when it happened. Some were ordinary days, unfinished projects, random photographs, conversations, and moments that seemed too small to keep. Somehow, they stayed. Tidak semua kenangan terasa penting ketika terjadi. Ada hari-hari biasa, tugas yang belum selesai, foto random, percakapan, dan momen kecil yang rasanya tidak perlu disimpan. Entah bagaimana, semuanya tetap tinggal.
